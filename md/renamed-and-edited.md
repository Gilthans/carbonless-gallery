# Winter timetable

The winter timetable runs from the last Sunday of October to the last Sunday of March.

## What changes

From the first winter Sunday:

- Fewer late departures on the Askøy route.
- The first Nordnes departure moves to 07:15.
- Weekend departures every 60 minutes.

## Why

Passenger numbers after 21:00 drop by about half in winter. Running fewer
late departures frees a crew for the morning peak.

## Ice

When the harbour at Nordnes freezes over, departures from Nordnes are
cancelled and passengers are sent to Strandkaien.

## Telling passengers

The winter timetable is posted at every port two weeks before it starts.
The app shows a banner on the first day.

## Decided

- The 23:40 from Strandkaien stays on Fridays.
- The office tells the integration partners a month ahead.
