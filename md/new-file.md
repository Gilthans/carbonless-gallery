# Accessibility on board

Every Tidewater vessel carries passengers who need help boarding. This page is
what crews do for them.

## Before boarding

Passengers can ask for help when they book, or at the gangway. The app marks
a booking that asked for help with a blue wheel icon on the purser's list.

- Lower the car-deck ramp to its shallowest setting.
- Keep the lift by the purser's desk clear.
- Reserve the two seats next to the lift.

## During the crossing

A deckhand checks on the passenger once during the crossing. On crossings
longer than forty minutes, the purser offers a drink from the kiosk.

## Arriving

Passengers who asked for help leave first, before the cars. The shift lead
radios the next port so a member of staff meets them on the quay.

## Guide dogs

Guide dogs travel free and may go anywhere a passenger can. Other dogs stay on
the car deck or in the dog room.

## Reporting

If something went wrong, note it in the shift report under **Passengers**. The
office calls the passenger within two working days.
