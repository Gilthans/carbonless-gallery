# Paper tickets

Paper tickets are sold at the kiosk on Strandkaien and on board from the purser's desk.

## Selling a paper ticket

1. Ask where the passenger is travelling.
2. Print the ticket from the desk printer.
3. Take payment by card. Cash only if the card terminal is down.

## Checking a paper ticket

Tear the stub at the gangway. Keep the stubs in the box under the desk.

## Refunds

Paper tickets are refunded only at the kiosk, never on board.

## End of the day

Count the stubs and write the total in the shift report.
