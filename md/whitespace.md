# Shift report

Fill in the shift report at the end of every shift.

- Crossings run
- Passengers carried

    Count from the scanner, not by hand. The scanner total
    includes children and bicycles.

- Delays and their reasons

Address lines in the report header:  
Tidewater Ferries  
Strandkaien 1

The report goes to the office by 20:00.


Late reports are chased the next morning.

```text
crossings:  14
passengers: 1932
delays:     2
```

The office archives reports for five years.
A copy stays on the vessel for one month.
