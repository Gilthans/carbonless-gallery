# Fog procedure

Added after push 1, so the since view shows it as a whole new file.

## Signals

In fog the bridge sounds one long blast every two minutes. Crews on deck keep
a lookout at the bow and report anything heard but not seen.

## Passengers

The purser asks passengers to stay inside and closes the doors to the open
deck. The app posts a fog notice on every departure from the affected port.

## When to stop

The shift lead stops the route when visibility at the pier is under 200 m.
