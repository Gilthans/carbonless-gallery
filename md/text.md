# Writing the release announcement

Every release of Tidewater gets a short announcement on the blog and in the app. This page is how we write it.

## Who reads it

Most readers are harbour masters and ferry operators who open the app twice a day, between sailings. They skim. They want to know whether anything they rely on has moved, and whether they need to tell their crews.

A second group reads every word: the integration partners who sync timetables into their own booking sites. They read the announcement next to the changelog, line by line, and test every example. For them a renamed field is a lost weekend of bookings.

## Tone

Write plainly. Say what changed, then why, then what the reader should do. Lead with the reader's task, not our team's work. Avoid "exciting" and "powerful"; let the change speak.

Use the second person. "You can now export a week of sailings" beats "Users are now able to export sailings for a week".

Name screens the way the app names them: **Departures**, not "the schedule page".

## Structure

Open with the one change most readers will notice. Put it in the first sentence, not the third.

Follow with the smaller changes as a short list. Each item names the screen it touches.

Close with anything that needs action: a setting to check, a field that is going away, a date.

Keep sentences short. If a sentence needs a semicolon, it probably wants to be two sentences.

## Length

Aim for under three hundred words.
A reader on a phone should finish it before the next sailing.
The announcement links to the full release notes; it does not replace them.

## Review

Every announcement is read by one person from support before it goes out. Support knows which wording turns into tickets.

Send the draft in the #releases channel at least a day before the release.

Tag the support lead by name.

## Timing

Announcements go out the morning after a release, local time in Bergen. Never on a Friday. If a release slips, the announcement slips with it. Do not publish an announcement for a release that is not live.

## Archive

Past announcements live in the blog archive. Link the previous one at the bottom so readers can follow the thread.

The archive is also where we look when a partner asks "when did this change?". Date **every** announcement.
